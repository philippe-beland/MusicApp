# MusicApp - User Manual

Welcome to MusicApp (better name to come)! This guide will help you get started and make the most of using the app.

MusicApp is an app for musicians to analyze music pieces. It will allow users to add time-based comments on tracks from either Apple Music, Spotify (coming later) and uploaded files (coming later).

## Limitations

In this pre-release version (0.1.0), this is pretty much a skin of Apple Music app, removing a bunch of features that are not necessarily useful for working with music, such as recommendations, playlists (will be added later), radio, stations. It takes for granted that the user have already added necessary tracks into their catalog, or that they know what artist/album/track they are looking for.

Please note that compilation albums, singles and live albums will not appear in the app. MusicApp processed mainly Full-feature albums. 

## Prerequisites:

- iPhone (iPad and Mac will come later)
- Minimum OS version: iOS 18.5

# 1. Getting Started

## 1.1 First Launch

1. **Open MusicApp** on your iPhone or iPad
2. **Grant Permissions**: When prompted, tap "Allow" to give MusicApp access to your Apple Music account. This is only necessary to playback music in the app.

# 6. Music Playback

### 6.1 Starting Playback

**Method 1: Play Entire Album**

- Tap the "Lecture" button to play all tracks

**Method 2: Play from Specific Track**

- Tap any track row to start from that track
- The app will play that track and all following tracks in the album

### 6.2 Playback Controls

A floating control bar appears at the bottom of the screen with:

- **Previous**: Skip to the previous track
- **Play/Pause**: Toggle playback
- **Next**: Skip to the next track

### 6.3 Queue Management

- When you start from a specific track, only that track and subsequent tracks are added to the queue
- The queue automatically ends when the album finishes
- There is no other music that gets automatically added to your Queue (as with Apple Music)

# 8. Troubleshooting

### 8.1 Common Issues

**"No music found"**

- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you're signed in to Apple Music
- Try restarting the app

**Playback not working**

- Make sure you have an active Apple Music subscription
- Check that your device volume is turned up
- Try tapping the play button again

**App crashes or freezes**

- Close the app completely and restart
- Check for iOS updates
- Restart your device if problems persist

### 8.2 Performance Tips

- **Use Wi-Fi** for faster music loading
- **Keep the app updated** for the best experience
